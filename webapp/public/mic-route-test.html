3b · Press reliability

Counts every transient louder than the spike threshold, with a 300 ms refractory so one press counts once. Press the button a known number of times (10 is good) and compare: 10 spikes near 1.0 is a healthy switch, 2 of 10 is an intermittent contact. Also try wiggling the 3.5 mm plug or flexing the cable without pressing — spikes there mean the connection is mechanically marginal, not the switch.

3c · Route roulette

Answers one question: does the real USB device ever actually bind? Talk or clap continuously for the whole sweep — your voice is the reference, because the iPad's speaker cannot be used (the adapter takes over audio output, so a tone would play out of its aux jack instead). It first calibrates on the built-in mic to check you are loud enough, then opens the Plugable input repeatedly. Each attempt is HEARS YOU (built-in mic, whatever the label claims) or DEAF (nothing acoustic reaches it — the only positive evidence the USB device is genuinely bound). If an attempt comes back DEAF the sweep stops and holds that stream open — press the button then and watch section 3b.
